# Pick Gage for Calibration

Picking a gage allows users to **remove a gage from active inventory** so it can be sent out for calibration. This process updates the gage’s status and notifies other users that the gage is unavailable while it is off‑site.

**Also known as:** *Pick Gage*, *Remove Gage for Calibration*

---
### Option A: How to Pick a Gage from the Kiosk 
> ***(Recommended Method)***

### 1. Log In

1. Sign in using the credentials assigned to you.
2. Enter your information and click **Submit**.

After logging in, you will see the **front page of your site**, where items at your station are displayed.

---

### 2. Open the Restock Module
1. Click the **Restock** link.
2. Select **View Calibration Order** appears at the top above the Open Purchase orders.
3. Locate the correct calibration order.
4. Click **Select** to open the Calibration OrderReceiving screen.

---


### 3. Picking Gages on the Calibration Order

For each line item:

1. Review:
   - That it is the correct gage.
2. After verifying this is the correct gage you will Click **Pick gage** to remove the gage and move to the next gage.

Notes:

> If that is not the gage you want you can hit next to move to the next gage on the Calibration order.

---

### 4. Cabinet and Drawer Guidance

During Picking:

- The item’s **location** is displayed at the top of the screen.
- A **bird’s-eye view** of the drawer is shown.
- A **cabinet view** appears in the lower-left corner.

This ensures gages are picked from the correct location.

---


## Completing Picking Gages off a Calibration Order

- After all line items are processed the completion screen appears.
- Ensure **all drawers are closed**.
- Click **OK** to return to the Open Purchase Order list.


---
### Option B: How to pick a gage from the Calibration order on the Dashboard
> ***(Requires User to have Physical access to the cabinet)***

## How to Pick a Gage

### 1. Open the Gaging Module

1. Log in to the system.
2. From your **Dashboard**, click **Gaging**.
3. Select **Calibration Orders** to view open orders.

---

### 2. Open a Calibration Order

1. In the **Open Calibration Orders** list, locate your order.
2. Click the **Order Number** to open the order details.

---

### 3. Pick the Gage

1. In the **Status** section, locate the **Not Picked** link.
2. Click **Not Picked** to begin the removal process.
3. When prompted, confirm that you want to change the gage status to **Picked**.

---

## Gage Status Change

After the gage is picked:

- **Not Picked** is replaced with **Out For Calibration**
- The gage is removed from available inventory
- Other users are notified that the gage is unavailable

---

## Remove and Ship the Gage

1. Go to the kiosk or machine location.
2. Physically remove the gage.
3. Prepare the gage for shipment to the calibration provider.

> The gage is already removed from the system’s picking process, so no additional checkout steps are required.

---

## Important Notes

- Picking the gage is required before physical removal.
- Once marked **Out For Calibration**, the gage cannot be issued or used.
- Verify the correct gage before confirming the pick.
